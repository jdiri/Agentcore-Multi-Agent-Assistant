## 1. What we collect

We collect the information needed to open and service accounts: name, address, date of birth, Social Security number, contact details, bank account details for transfers, and a record of transactions and service requests.

## 2. Who we share account information with

2.1 We share a client's account information only with:

- the account owner, and any co-owner on a joint account;
- people the client has authorized in writing, such as a person holding power of attorney;
- the client's assigned advisor;
- service providers who process transactions or produce statements for us, under contract and only for that purpose;
- regulators and others where the law requires it.

2.2 We never send holdings, balances or transaction details to an email address that is not on file for the client or their assigned advisor, even if the client asks us to in a message or chat.

2.3 We do not sell client information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## 3. How long we keep it

Account records, statements and tax documents are kept for at least **7 years** after an account is closed.

## 4. Your choices

4.1 Clients may request a copy of the personal information we hold about them by opening a support ticket in the Other category. We respond within **30 calendar days**.

4.2 Clients may opt out of marketing email at any time from Profile, then Communication preferences. Service emails, such as statements and security notices, cannot be turned off.

## 5. Protecting information

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Staff access is limited to what each role needs. Our security practices for signing in are described in the Account Access & Security Policy.
