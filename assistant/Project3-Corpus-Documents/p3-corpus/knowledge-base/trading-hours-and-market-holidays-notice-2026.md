Version: 2026

## 1. Daily cutoff

Orders to buy, sell or exchange fund shares that are received before **4 p.m. Eastern time** on a business day receive that day's closing price (NAV). Orders received later receive the next business day's price.

## 2. Market holidays in 2026

The firm does not process trades on these days. Orders placed on a holiday receive the next business day's price.

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | Thursday, January 1 |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | Monday, January 19 |
| Presidents' Day | Monday, February 16 |
| Good Friday | Friday, April 3 |
| Memorial Day | Monday, May 25 |
| Juneteenth | Friday, June 19 |
| Independence Day (observed) | Friday, July 3 |
| Labor Day | Monday, September 7 |
| Thanksgiving Day | Thursday, November 26 |
| Christmas Day | Friday, December 25 |

## 3. Early-close days in 2026

On these days, markets close early and the order cutoff moves to **1 p.m. Eastern time**:

- Friday, November 27 (the day after Thanksgiving)
- Thursday, December 24 (Christmas Eve)

## 4. Client services hours

Client services is available by phone Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern time, except on the market holidays above. Support tickets can be opened at any time; response times in the Support Ticket Handling Policy count business days only.
