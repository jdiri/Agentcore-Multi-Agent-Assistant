# Automatic Investment Plan FAQ

Effective: 2025-06-01

**What is an automatic investment plan?**
A standing instruction to move a set amount from your bank account into one or more funds on a regular schedule.

**How often can it run?**
Every two weeks, monthly or quarterly. You choose the date of the first draw.

**What is the minimum?**
$50 per fund per draw. The fund's minimum initial investment must already be met, so a plan can only buy funds you already own.

**How do I change or stop a plan?**
Go to Account, then Automatic investments. Changes and cancellations must be made at least 3 business days before the next draw date to apply to it.

**Can I pause a plan?**
Yes, for up to 6 months. The plan restarts automatically on the first scheduled draw date after the pause ends.

**What happens if a draw fails?**
If your bank returns a draw, the purchase is reversed and the returned-deposit fee applies (see the Fee Schedule FAQ). After two failed draws in a row, the plan is suspended and you receive an email. You can restart it from the same page.

**Do automatic purchases count toward the frequent trading limit?**
No. Automatic investment plan purchases are exempt under the Frequent Trading Policy.

**Will I get a trade confirmation for each draw?**
No. Automatic investment plan purchases appear on your next statement instead of generating a separate confirmation.
