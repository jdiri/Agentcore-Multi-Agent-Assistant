# Contributions & Deposits Policy

Document ID: POL-DEP
Version: 2.1
Effective: 2025-06-01
Supersedes: 2.0
Owner: Client Services Operations

## 1. Scope

This policy covers adding money to an account. Moving assets from another institution is covered by the Account Transfer Policy.

## 2. Accepted methods

2.1 **Electronic bank transfer (ACH)** from a bank account in the client's name.

2.2 **Check** made payable to the firm, drawn on a U.S. bank account in the client's name.

2.3 **Wire transfer** from a U.S. bank.

2.4 The firm does not accept cash, money orders, traveler's checks or third-party checks (a check written to someone else and signed over to the client). These are returned by mail.

## 3. When the money is invested

3.1 Purchases funded by ACH are invested at the closing price on the business day the bank transfer is initiated, if the request is received before 4 p.m. Eastern time.

3.2 Checks are invested at the closing price on the business day they are received and processed.

3.3 Wires received before 4 p.m. Eastern time are invested the same business day.

## 4. Holds on newly deposited money

4.1 Shares bought with an ACH deposit can be exchanged right away, but the proceeds cannot be withdrawn from the firm for **7 calendar days**, while the bank transfer clears.

4.2 Shares bought with a check have the same restriction for **7 business days**.

4.3 Wired money has no hold.

## 5. IRA contributions

5.1 Each IRA contribution must name the tax year it is for. Contributions for the previous tax year are accepted from January 1 until the federal tax filing deadline.

5.2 The IRS sets an annual contribution limit. The firm rejects any contribution that would take the account owner over their limit, based on the contributions recorded at the firm. The firm cannot see contributions made at other institutions and does not give tax advice.

## 6. Returned deposits

6.1 If an ACH deposit or check is returned unpaid, the purchase it funded is reversed and a $20 returned-deposit fee is charged (see the Fee Schedule FAQ).
