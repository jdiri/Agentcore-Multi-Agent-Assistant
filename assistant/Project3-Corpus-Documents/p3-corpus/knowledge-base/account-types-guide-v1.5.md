## 1. Individual account

Owned by one person. Only the owner can place trades or request withdrawals.

## 2. Joint account

2.1 Owned by two people as joint tenants with rights of survivorship.

2.2 Either owner can place trades, request statements and open support tickets. Withdrawals by check are made payable to both owners.

2.3 Adding or removing an owner changes the account registration, so it requires opening a new account and transferring the holdings.

## 3. Traditional IRA

3.1 An individual retirement account. Contributions may be tax-deductible, and earnings grow tax-deferred until withdrawn.

3.2 Contributions must name a tax year (see the Contributions & Deposits Policy). Withdrawals are tax-reportable.

## 4. Roth IRA

4.1 An individual retirement account funded with after-tax money. Qualified withdrawals are tax-free.

4.2 Eligibility to contribute depends on income limits set by the IRS. The firm does not check income eligibility and does not give tax advice.

## 5. Custodial account (UTMA/UGMA)

5.1 Opened by an adult custodian for a minor. The assets belong to the minor, but the custodian manages the account.

5.2 Control passes to the minor at the age set by the state the account was opened under. The custodian is notified 90 days before that date.

## 6. Opening an account

6.1 Accounts are opened online and take about 10 minutes. There is no minimum balance to open an account, but each fund has a minimum initial investment (see the Fund Minimums & Investment Options FAQ).

6.2 A client can hold more than one account of each type.

## 7. Not covered in this guide

Transfer-on-death and beneficiary arrangements are handled separately and are not covered in this guide.
