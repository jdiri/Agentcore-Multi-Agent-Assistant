# Account Transfer Policy

Document ID: POL-TRF
Version: 3.2
Effective: 2026-04-01
Supersedes: 3.1
Owner: Client Services Operations

## 1. Scope

This policy covers moving assets between an account at the firm and an account at another financial institution, in either direction. It applies to individual, joint, IRA and custodial accounts. It does not cover moves between two accounts at the firm (see the Fund Minimums & Investment Options FAQ for exchanges) or transfers of inherited assets, which client services handles case by case.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **Incoming transfer.** Assets move from another institution into an account at the firm.

2.2 **Outgoing transfer.** Assets move from an account at the firm to another institution.

2.3 **ACATS transfer.** A transfer between two brokerage firms that both participate in the Automated Customer Account Transfer Service. Most brokerage accounts qualify.

2.4 **Non-ACATS transfer.** A transfer involving an institution that does not use ACATS, such as many banks, insurance companies and some fund companies. These are processed manually.

2.5 **In-kind transfer.** Holdings move as they are, without being sold.

2.6 **Cash transfer.** The sending institution sells the holdings and sends the proceeds.

## 3. Starting a transfer

3.1 Incoming transfers are started at the firm by submitting a transfer request online. The client provides the other institution's name, the account number there, and a recent statement from that account.

3.2 Outgoing transfers are started at the receiving institution, not at the firm. The receiving institution sends the request to us.

3.3 The account registration (owner names and account type) must match on both sides. A transfer from an individual account into a joint account, or from a Traditional IRA into a Roth IRA, is not a transfer and will be rejected.

## 4. Timelines

4.1 Incoming ACATS transfers normally complete in **3 to 6 business days** from the date we receive a complete request.

4.2 Outgoing ACATS transfers normally complete in **3 to 6 business days** from the date the receiving institution submits the request.

4.3 Non-ACATS transfers normally take **2 to 3 weeks**, because the other institution processes them by hand.

4.4 Timelines are paused while a request is missing information. We notify the client by email within 2 business days if anything is missing.

## 5. Documentation

5.1 A **Medallion signature guarantee** is required for any non-ACATS transfer of more than **$250,000**. Below that amount, the client's electronic signature on the online request is sufficient.

5.2 Custodial account transfers require the custodian's signature. The minor cannot sign.

## 6. Partial transfers

6.1 Clients may transfer part of an account. Each fund position being transferred must be worth at least $1,000, or be transferred in full.

## 7. Fees

7.1 The firm does not charge a fee for incoming or outgoing transfers.

7.2 For incoming transfers of $25,000 or more, the firm reimburses the fee the sending institution charges to close or transfer the account, up to **$150** per account. The client requests reimbursement by opening a support ticket with a copy of the fee notice within 60 days of the transfer completing.

## 8. Status and delays

8.1 Clients can see transfer status online. A transfer not complete within the timeline in Section 4 can be raised as a support ticket in the Transfers category.
