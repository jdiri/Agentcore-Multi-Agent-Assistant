**Is there an annual account fee?**
Yes. Each account is charged a $25 annual account service fee in December. The fee is waived if your combined balance across all of your accounts at the firm is $10,000 or more, or if you are enrolled in electronic delivery of statements and documents.

**Do you charge commissions to buy or sell your funds?**
No. There is no commission, purchase fee or redemption fee for buying, selling or exchanging Lanternfield funds.

**What is an expense ratio, and will I see it on my bill?**
An expense ratio is the fund's yearly operating cost, shown as a percentage of the fund's assets. It is taken out of the fund itself and is already reflected in the fund's share price (NAV). You are never billed for it separately, and it will not appear as a line on your statement.

**Is there a fee to talk to my advisor?**
No. Access to your assigned advisor is included with every account at no additional charge.

**Do you charge for wires?**
Incoming wires are free. Each outgoing wire costs $10.

**What if a deposit from my bank is returned?**
A returned ACH deposit or a returned check costs $20, because the purchase it funded has to be reversed.

**Can I get a check sent overnight?**
Yes, for $15. Standard mail delivery of a check is free.

**Do you charge to close an account or transfer it out?**
No. The firm does not charge to close an account or to transfer assets to another institution. The receiving institution may charge its own fees. For reimbursement of fees another firm charges when you move assets to us, see the Account Transfer Policy.

**Is there a fee for paper statements?**
There is no separate paper statement fee. However, choosing electronic delivery is one of the two ways to have the annual account service fee waived.
