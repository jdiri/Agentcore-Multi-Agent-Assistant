# Address & Profile Changes FAQ

**How do I change my mailing address?**
Sign in and go to Profile, then Personal information. Address changes take effect immediately. We send a confirmation letter to both the old and the new address.

**Is anything restricted after I change my address?**
Yes. For 15 calendar days after an address change, we will not mail a check to the new address. You can still receive money by bank transfer or wire to a bank account already on file. See the Withdrawals & Redemptions Policy.

**How do I change my email address?**
Go to Profile, then Personal information. We send a confirmation link to the new address and a notice to the old address. The change takes effect when you click the link, which expires after 24 hours.

**How do I change my phone number?**
Go to Profile, then Personal information. You will be asked for an MFA code first. If the phone number is the one you use for text message codes, set up the new number before removing the old one.

**How do I change my name?**
Name changes require a document showing the change, such as a marriage certificate or court order. Upload it from Profile, then Personal information, then Request a name change. Name changes are completed within 5 business days.

**Can I add a second owner to my account?**
Not to an existing account. See the Account Types Guide for joint accounts.

**I cannot make the change online. What should I do?**
Open a support ticket in the Profile Changes category and describe the change you need.
