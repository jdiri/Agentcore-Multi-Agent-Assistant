# Advisor Services Overview

Document ID: OVR-ADV
Version: 1.2
Effective: 2025-10-01
Supersedes: 1.1
Owner: Advice Services

## 1. Your assigned advisor

1.1 Every client is assigned an advisor when their first account is opened. The advisor's name appears in Profile.

1.2 Access to your advisor is included with every account at no additional charge.

## 2. Reaching your advisor

2.1 Clients can send their advisor a secure message from the website, or ask the digital assistant to draft an email to their advisor.

2.2 Emails drafted by the digital assistant are reviewed by a person before they are sent. The client is told when an email is waiting for review and when it has been sent.

2.3 The digital assistant emails only the advisor on record for the signed-in client. It does not send account information to any other address.

2.4 Advisors respond within **1 business day**. Meetings by phone or video can be booked through the advisor.

## 3. What advisors do

3.1 Advisors give personalized investment advice: choosing funds, setting an asset mix, planning for retirement goals and reviewing a portfolio.

3.2 Advisors do not give tax or legal advice.

## 4. What the digital assistant does not do

4.1 The digital assistant answers questions about policies and account information, and can take service actions such as opening a support ticket. It does **not** give investment advice, recommend specific funds, or say whether to buy, sell or hold anything.

4.2 When a client asks for investment advice, the assistant declines and offers to connect the client with their advisor.

## 5. Changing advisors

5.1 A client may request a different advisor by opening a support ticket in the Other category. Changes take effect within 5 business days, and the client receives an email with the new advisor's name.
