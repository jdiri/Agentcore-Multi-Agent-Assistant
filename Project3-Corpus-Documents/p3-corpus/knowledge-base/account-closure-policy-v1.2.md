# Account Closure Policy

Document ID: POL-CLS
Version: 1.2
Effective: 2025-01-15
Supersedes: 1.1
Owner: Client Services Operations

## 1. Before an account can close

1.1 The account must have a zero balance. The client first sells all holdings and withdraws the proceeds, or transfers the holdings to another institution (see the Account Transfer Policy).

1.2 There must be no pending transactions, including an automatic investment plan draw scheduled in the next 3 business days. Cancel any automatic investment plan first.

1.3 Any hold on recently deposited money must have ended (see the Contributions & Deposits Policy, Section 4).

## 2. Closing the account

2.1 Individual and joint accounts with a zero balance can be closed online from Account, then Close account. Either owner of a joint account can close it.

2.2 IRAs are closed by completing a full withdrawal request online. Withdrawals from an IRA are tax-reportable (see the Withdrawals & Redemptions Policy, Section 7).

2.3 There is no fee to close an account.

## 3. After closure

3.1 Statements, confirmations and tax forms for a closed account remain available online for 7 years.

3.2 If a distribution or other payment arrives after closure, it is sent to the client by check at their address of record.

3.3 An account closed in the last **90 days** can be reopened by opening a support ticket in the Other category. After 90 days, the client opens a new account.
