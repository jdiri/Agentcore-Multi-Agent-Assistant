**What is the minimum to invest in a fund?**
The minimum initial investment is $1,000 for index funds and for the Federal Money Market Fund, and $3,000 for actively managed funds. Each fund's minimum is shown in its details.

**Is there a minimum for additional purchases?**
Yes. Once you own a fund, each additional purchase must be at least $50.

**What is an exchange?**
An exchange sells shares of one Lanternfield fund and uses the proceeds to buy another, in the same account, in a single request. Exchanges are free.

**When does an exchange happen?**
An exchange requested before 4 p.m. Eastern time on a business day uses that day's closing price for both the sale and the purchase. Requests after the cutoff use the next business day's prices.

**Do exchanges have to meet the fund minimum?**
Yes. An exchange into a fund you do not already own must meet that fund's minimum initial investment.

**Can I exchange between two of my accounts?**
No. Moving money between two different accounts, such as from an individual account into an IRA, is a withdrawal from one and a contribution to the other, and may have tax consequences.

**What is the difference between an index fund and an actively managed fund?**
An index fund tries to match the performance of a market index by holding the securities in it. An actively managed fund has managers who choose investments in an attempt to do better than a benchmark. Actively managed funds generally have higher expense ratios.

**Where can I see a fund's past returns?**
Each fund's details show its quarterly total returns. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

**Can you tell me which fund I should buy?**
The digital assistant does not recommend funds. Your assigned advisor can help you choose (see the Advisor Services Overview).

**Do exchanges count toward the frequent trading limit?**
Yes. An exchange into a fund and back out within 30 calendar days is a round trip under the Frequent Trading Policy.
