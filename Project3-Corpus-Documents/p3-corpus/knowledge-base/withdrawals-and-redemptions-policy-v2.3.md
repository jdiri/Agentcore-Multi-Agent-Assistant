# Withdrawals & Redemptions Policy

Document ID: POL-WDR
Version: 2.3
Effective: 2025-08-15
Supersedes: 2.2
Owner: Client Services Operations

## 1. Scope

This policy covers selling fund shares (a redemption) and moving the proceeds out of the firm. Exchanges between funds at the firm are covered in the Fund Minimums & Investment Options FAQ.

## 2. Order cutoff and pricing

2.1 A sell order received before **4 p.m. Eastern time** on a business day is priced at that day's closing NAV. Orders received after the cutoff, or on a market holiday, are priced at the next business day's closing NAV. On early-close days the cutoff is 1 p.m. Eastern time (see the Trading Hours & Market Holidays Notice).

2.2 A sell order cannot be cancelled after the cutoff for the day it was placed.

## 3. Settlement

3.1 Redemptions settle **one business day** after the trade date (T+1). Proceeds can be sent only after settlement.

## 4. Receiving the proceeds

| Method | When it arrives | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic bank transfer (ACH) | 1 to 2 business days after settlement | Free |
| Wire | Same business day as settlement, if requested before 2 p.m. Eastern time | $10 |
| Check by standard mail | 5 to 7 business days after settlement | Free |
| Check by overnight delivery | Next business day after it is mailed | $15 |

4.1 Proceeds are sent only to a bank account in the client's name or to the client's address of record.

4.2 A check will not be mailed to an address that was changed in the last **15 calendar days**. During that period, proceeds can be sent by ACH or wire to a bank account that was already on file.

## 5. Holds on recent deposits

5.1 Proceeds from shares bought with a recent ACH deposit or check are subject to the hold in the Contributions & Deposits Policy, Section 4.

## 6. Large redemptions

6.1 A redemption of more than **$100,000** sent by check, or sent to a bank account added in the last 30 days, requires the client to confirm the request by phone before it is released.

## 7. IRA withdrawals

7.1 Withdrawals from an IRA are tax-reportable. The client chooses a federal withholding election on the withdrawal request. The firm does not give tax advice and recommends clients speak with a tax professional before withdrawing from an IRA.
