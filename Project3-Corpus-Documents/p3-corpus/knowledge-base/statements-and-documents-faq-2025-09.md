**When are statements available?**
Quarterly statements are available within 10 business days after the end of each calendar quarter. If your account had any activity in a month, you also receive a monthly statement for that month, available within 10 business days after month end.

**How do I switch to electronic delivery?**
Sign in and go to Profile, then Communication preferences, then choose Electronic for statements and documents. The change applies to the next statement produced. Electronic delivery also waives the annual account service fee (see the Fee Schedule FAQ).

**How long are statements available online?**
Statements, trade confirmations and tax forms stay available online for 7 years, including for accounts that have since been closed.

**Can I get a paper copy of an older statement?**
Yes. Open a support ticket in the Statements & Documents category and list the months you need. Copies are mailed within 10 business days at no charge.

**When will I get a trade confirmation?**
A confirmation is available online within 1 business day of each purchase, sale or exchange, except purchases made through an automatic investment plan, which appear on your next statement instead.

**Why does my statement show a different value than the website?**
A statement shows values as of the last business day of the period it covers. The website shows values as of the most recent closing price. If you made a transaction after the statement period ended, the two will differ.

**When are tax forms available?**
Form 1099-DIV and Form 1099-B are available by February 15 for the previous tax year. Form 5498, which reports IRA contributions, is available by May 31. You receive an email when each form is ready if you use electronic delivery.

**My statement is missing. What should I do?**
First check that electronic delivery is turned on and that your email address in Profile is current. If a statement older than 10 business days past the period end is still missing, open a support ticket in the Statements & Documents category.
